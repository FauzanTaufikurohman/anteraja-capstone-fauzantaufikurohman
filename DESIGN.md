# Design Direction: Anteraja Landing Page

## Brand Identity
- **Product:** Anteraja (PT. Tri Adi Bersama)
- **Sector:** Online Logistics, Express Delivery, and Supply Chain Solutions
- **Target Audience:** Retail consumers, online shoppers, UMKM/e-commerce sellers, and corporate enterprises
- **Reference Site:** https://anteraja.id/id/

## Visual Personality
- Reliable, fast, friendly, and technology-driven
- Clean, high-contrast, modern Indonesian logistics brand
- Human-centric without artificial tech clichés (no generic AI orbs, neon grids, or robot graphics)

## Palette
- **Primary Accent:** `#ED0677` (Anteraja Magenta - used for primary CTAs, active indicators, brand accent)
- **Primary Dark:** `#212121` (High-contrast text, dark headings, meets WCAG AAA on white)
- **Secondary Surface:** `#FAEFF1` (Warm soft rose tint for widget bar and subtle accents)
- **Background Light:** `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA` (Clean neutral canvas)
- **Border / Divider:** `#E6E6E6` and `#F0E5E7` (Subtle boundary definitions)
- **Muted Text:** `#666666` (Meets WCAG AA 4.5:1 on white surfaces)

## Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** 'Open Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif
- **Font Weights:** 400 (Body text), 600 (Semibold navigation/labels), 700 (Bold headings & buttons)
- **No decorative monospace** outside of actual code contexts

## Dials
- **ENERGY:** 2 (Balanced - direct, commercial, trustworthy)
- **RHYTHM:** 2 (Structured - alternation between interactive widgets, cards, banners, and logos)
- **MOTION:** 1 (Subtle - crisp hover states and tab transitions, no endless automated bouncing loops)
